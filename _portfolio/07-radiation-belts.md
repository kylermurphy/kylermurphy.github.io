Much of my research has focused on the ultra-low-frequency (ULF) waves that transport and accelerate radiation-belt electrons, the population that damages satellites.

- A new four-component, L*-dependent radial diffusion model driven by solar wind and magnetospheric conditions, built for use in radiation-belt forecasting ([Murphy et al., 2023, *Space Weather*](https://doi.org/10.1029/2023SW003440)).
- A statistical framework for quantifying loss and acceleration of relativistic electrons during storms.
- Co-author on *Nature Physics* and *Nature Communications* papers explaining the third Van Allen belt and the apparent "impenetrable barrier" to ultra-relativistic electrons.
