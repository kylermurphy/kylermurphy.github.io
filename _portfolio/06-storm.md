STORM (Solar-Terrestrial Observer for the Response of the Magnetosphere) is a mission concept to image the solar-wind–magnetosphere system globally. I led the analysis of what the proposed instruments would actually be able to see from the planned orbit, and how often: the target and science visibility that links the science goals to orbit and instrument requirements.

Published in *Frontiers in Astronomy and Space Sciences* ([Murphy et al., 2024](https://doi.org/10.3389/fspas.2024.1394655)).
