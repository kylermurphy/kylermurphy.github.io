When the pandemic cancelled scientific meetings, the Magnetosphere Online Seminar Series gave the community a regular place to present and discuss new work. Seminars run on Zoom and are live-streamed to YouTube, where [past talks](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCNlOK9mCmI3V111EHQRCuEQ) stay available.
