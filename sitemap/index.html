    portfolio

    MLTDM: Machine-learning thermospheric density model

    Published: September 27, 2026

    A random-forest model of storm-time atmospheric neutral density, published in Space Weather (2025) and released as an open, installable Python package with a worked example notebook.

    CONTIGO: satellite-derived atmospheric density from orbits

    Published: September 27, 2026

    A modular framework that turns spacecraft orbit data into energy dissipation rates and effective atmospheric density, validated against the Orekit flight-dynamics library.

    ml_fw: an ML toolkit for space-weather forecasting

    Published: September 27, 2026

    Reusable building blocks for time-series ML in heliophysics: lagged features, tuning, diagnostics, and ARIMA-based perturbed-input ensembles for forecast uncertainty.

    GMAG: ground magnetometer data and induced electric fields

    Published: September 27, 2026

    An open-source package that downloads and loads data from several ground-magnetometer arrays into one pandas interface, now with tools to compute geoelectric fields for space-weather hazard work.

    JB2008 in Python, ~11x faster

    Published: September 27, 2026

    Vectorized and parallelized the Python port of the JB2008 empirical density model, cutting ~70,000 predictions from ~8 minutes to ~40 seconds.

    STORM mission concept: what a global imager would see

    Published: September 27, 2026

    Lead author of the target and science-visibility analysis for STORM, a proposed mission to image the whole magnetosphere at once.

    Radiation belts, ULF waves, and radial diffusion

    Published: September 27, 2026

    Over a decade of work on how geomagnetic storms energize and deplete Earth’s radiation belts, including a new operational-ready radial diffusion model.

    Magnetosphere Online Seminar Series

    Published: September 27, 2026

    An international online seminar series that kept the space physics community connected when conferences were cancelled during COVID-19.
